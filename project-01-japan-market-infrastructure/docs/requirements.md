# Project Requirements

## Project Background

A Taiwan-based B2B SaaS startup is preparing to expand its business into the Japanese market.

The company needs a basic AWS infrastructure in the Tokyo Region to run its existing web application for Japanese business customers.

The initial infrastructure should be simple, secure, cost-conscious, and designed so that it can be expanded as the business grows.

## Business Requirements

- The initial service will support approximately 100–300 users in Japan.
- The web application must be accessible to Japanese business customers over the internet.
- Application data must not be directly accessible from the public internet.
- Operating costs should remain low during the initial market launch.
- Short periods of downtime are acceptable during the early stage.
- The infrastructure should be able to evolve as the Japanese business grows.
- The infrastructure must be reproducible using Terraform.
